What is Location Quotient (LQ)?

LQ measures regional concentration of industry employment compared to the national average. An LQ ≥ 1.0 indicates a regional specialization and competitive advantage. The dashboard filters, quadrants, and interactive charts below let you explore these dynamics across all 10 Indiana SBDC regions.
