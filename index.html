SBDC Executive Playbook & Regional Insights

Regional Dynamics

Indiana's SBDC territories demonstrate high regional specialization. While Central Indiana acts as a major service and healthcare hub (accounting for over 1 million jobs), adjacent SBDC regions are anchored strongly in manufacturing. Advisors should focus on localized supply chains specific to the region's core cluster specializations.

Location Quotient (LQ)

An LQ > 1.0 signifies that the region has a higher concentration of employment in that cluster than the national average, indicating unique regional strengths. Sustained wages are heavily aligned with high-specialization Star clusters, offering strategic expansion and investment opportunities.

Advisor Strategic Actions

Identify Stars to support high-growth capital injection. Leverage up-and-coming Emerging clusters to foster entrepreneurial networks. Guide businesses in Mature sectors toward tech adoption, and help legacy firms in Transforming quadrants adapt to transitioning markets.
